⌚ Why is Garmin Watch showing "No Target" for Pace?
When Intervals.icu syncs workouts to Garmin watches, Garmin's API requires your Threshold Pace to calculate targets:
  • In Intervals.icu, go to Settings → Sport Settings → Running and ensure your Threshold Pace is set (e.g. 4:30 /km). Without this, Intervals.icu drops pace targets on Garmin sync.
  • In the workout editor dialog, verify the Target selector is set to Pace (or Auto).
  • If you already synced the workout, delete it from your calendar and re-add it to trigger a fresh export to Garmin Connect.
